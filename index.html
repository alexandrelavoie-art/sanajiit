What are we trying to achieve?
🎯
Step 1 — Purpose
Learning Objectives
  1. Build confidence in taking measurements and using common carpentry tools safely and accurately.
  2. Develop foundational carpentry skills through practical exercises and guided projects, emphasizing repetition of essential skills.
  3. Adopt safe and professional work habits consistent with real construction site expectations.
  4. Strengthen mathematical skills related to measurement and material estimation, and gain a first exposure to blueprint reading.
  5. Foster employability skills — organization, teamwork, and the habit of honest self-evaluation.
